Method

Based on the paper Cross-Cohort Coronary Artery Disease Prediction Using Interpretable and Calibration-Aware Machine Learning (Sahani, Ojha, Khan; mentor Mrs. Shanu Verma, Galgotias College of Engineering and Technology).

  • Development: Cleveland (303 records). L2 logistic regression and a 500-tree random forest, preprocessing fitted inside each training fold, no oversampling.
  • External test: 617 records from Hungary, Switzerland, Long Beach VA, no refitting.
  • Portable model: 10 predictors; ST slope, vessel count and thalassemia dropped because they are 14-99% missing elsewhere.
  • Prevalence adjustment: the app shifts the logit by log-odds(target prevalence) minus log-odds(Cleveland prevalence). It corrects average risk only, not poor ranking.
  • Explanations: bars show coefficient x standardised value of the logistic model. They describe associations, not causes.

Outcome: angiographic CAD (>50% narrowing) in angiography-referred patients. This is not a screening tool and not a heart-attack predictor.
